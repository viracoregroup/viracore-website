Our Team

The people behind ViraCore

Work with us →

ViraCore is led by a team of operators, veterans, and industry professionals who have built careers delivering at the highest levels. Our founding team brings decades of combined experience across some of the most demanding environments in business and service.

Benjimin Aggrey
Chief Executive Officer

Benjimin is a U.S. Navy veteran with six years of service, where he served as a Supervisor and Training Technician within the Navy's medical technology sector — leading project management and technical operations in high-stakes environments. Since transitioning from active duty, he has built expertise in contract writing, bid analysis, and complex procurement navigation. He holds an MBA, an active Secret Security Clearance, and is a VCU graduate. Benjimin's SDVOSB eligibility and hands-on operational leadership anchor ViraCore's government contracting capability.

Kenneth Somuah
Co-Founder

Kenneth brings a rare blend of operational and technical fluency to ViraCore. He spent several years supporting JPMorgan's data center operations as a Data Center Team Lead, followed by an extended run building and directing warehouse, logistics, and storefront operations for the world's largest sneaker and apparel retailer — managing large-scale personnel and multi-site, high-volume supply chains. He has also supported the FDIC on IT deployment and refresh programs, and worked in operational and personnel management with the CFPB across multiple financial institutions. A VCU graduate, Kenneth leads ViraCore's growth strategy, technology adoption, and client relationships.
